Try them live: press S now to open this speaker view, Esc for the overview, Alt and click to zoom into a key. P in preview writes the PDF; in a static build it opens the print dialog. Scroll view is a URL parameter, not a key: open index.html?view=scroll. Technique: a custom fragment style, ink-in, defined in styles.css.
