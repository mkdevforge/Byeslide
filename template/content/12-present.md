# Present from the browser

These keys work right now. In Teams, share this window and keep the speaker view on your own screen.

- **S**, Speaker view: notes, timer and the next slide
- **F**, Full screen: press Esc to leave
- **Esc**, Overview: every slide at once
- **Alt**, Zoom: Alt and click on any detail
- **P**, PDF: in preview, writes `dist/deck.pdf`
- **?view=scroll**, Scroll view: read it as one long page

## Speaker notes

Try them live: press S now to open this speaker view, Esc for the overview, Alt and click to zoom into a key. P in preview writes the PDF; in a static build it opens the print dialog. Scroll view is a URL parameter, not a key: open index.html?view=scroll. Technique: a custom fragment style, ink-in, defined in styles.css.
