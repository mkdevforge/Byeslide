Exit ticket

Write your answers on a slip of paper. We look from the Northern Hemisphere.

  1. Where does the Moon's light come from?

    From the Sun: moonlight is sunlight bouncing off the Moon.

  2. Earth is between the Sun and the Moon. Which phase do we see?

    Full moon: we see the whole sunlit half.

  3. The left half of the Moon is lit. Is it waxing or waning?

    Waning: it is a third quarter, and the lit part is shrinking.

About 3 minutes. Pupils answer on a slip of paper or a sticky note. Give them 2 minutes, collect the slips, then click once for each answer and go through them together. Question 1 checks the first goal, question 2 the model, and question 3 the phase names. Sort the slips into three piles: got it, nearly, not yet. Pupils who write "Earth's shadow" anywhere still hold the main wrong idea: start the next lesson with the model again.
