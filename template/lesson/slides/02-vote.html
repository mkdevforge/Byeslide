Hands up for A, B or C. We find out who is right later in the lesson.

About 4 minutes. Read the three explanations aloud. Give pupils 30 seconds to choose on their own, then ask for hands for A, B and C, and click a choice once for each hand. Shift-click or right-click takes a vote away if you miscount. Do not say which is right: slide 8 comes back to this vote. Many pupils, and many adults, choose A. Earth's shadow is the most common wrong idea about the phases. B is common with younger pupils. C is correct. Ask one pupil from each group to say why they chose it, and write the reasons on the board so you can come back to them.
