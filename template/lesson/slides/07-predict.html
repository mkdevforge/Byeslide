What will the Moon look like in 4 days?

Tonight the Moon is a waxing crescent. New moon was 3 days ago.

A
B
C
D

BFirst quarter, lit on the right. 3 + 4 = 7 days after new moon, so the Moon has gone about a quarter of the way around Earth.

About 3 minutes. Pupils hold up 1 to 4 fingers for A to D, or write the letter on a mini whiteboard. Ask two pupils to explain their choice before you click. Then click to light up the answer. A is a waning crescent: lit on the wrong side. C is a third quarter: also lit on the wrong side, and about three weeks after new moon. D is a full moon: about two weeks after new moon. Pupils who choose C usually know "half lit" but not which side. Remind them: in the Northern Hemisphere the light grows from the right. If pupils ask about a week: a week after a waxing crescent the Moon is usually a waxing gibbous.
