# The eight phases, in order

As seen from the Northern Hemisphere. Days are counted from new moon.

A drawing of each phase, one per click:

1. New moon, Day 0
2. Waxing crescent, Day 4
3. First quarter, Day 7
4. Waxing gibbous, Day 11
5. Full moon, Day 15
6. Waning gibbous, Day 18
7. Third quarter, Day 22
8. Waning crescent, Day 26

On the next click, four words:

- **Waxing**: growing
- **Waning**: shrinking
- **Crescent**: less than half lit
- **Gibbous**: more than half lit

## Speaker notes

About 4 minutes. Click once for each phase, and ask the class to guess the name before you click. Then click once more for the four words. Waxing means growing and waning means shrinking. Crescent means less than half lit, gibbous means more than half lit. "First quarter" confuses pupils because the Moon looks half lit: the name means the Moon has gone a quarter of the way around Earth. Third quarter is also called last quarter. The dark patches stay in the same place in every drawing, because the same side of the Moon always faces Earth. The days are rounded: a full cycle is 29.5 days.
