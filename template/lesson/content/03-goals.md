# By the end of this lesson you can…

- say where the Moon's light comes from
- name the eight phases of the Moon in order
- use a model to explain why the phases happen
- explain why Earth's shadow does not cause the phases

Each goal has a small Moon next to it, a little fuller each time: waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon.

## Speaker notes

About 1 minute. Read the goals aloud. Point out that the Moon next to each goal grows a little more each time: by the end of the lesson pupils will know why. Come back to this slide at the end, or ask pupils to rate themselves from 1 to 4 on each goal on their exit ticket.
