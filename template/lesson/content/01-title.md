# Why does the Moon have phases?

Science: Earth and space

Picture: a large Moon, mostly lit on the right (a waxing gibbous). When the slide opens it grows from a thin crescent.

## Speaker notes

About 1 minute. Read the question aloud and let it hang for a moment. Ask: "Who has noticed the Moon looking different on different nights?" Take two or three answers, but do not explain yet. Pupils will vote on an explanation next. The lesson takes about 45 minutes. For the activity on slide 10 you need a lamp without a shade, and a pale ball on a pencil for each pupil or pair.
