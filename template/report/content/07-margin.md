# Operating margin rose 1.3 points to 13.1%, led by software mix

Software, cheaper freight and July's gateway price rise added 2.0 points. Less hardware volume and new hires took 0.7 back.

A bridge from Q2 to Q3 operating margin. The two totals show first; each click adds the next step. Software mix is the step that matters.

| Step | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Q2 2026 | 11.8% |
| Software mix | +1.1 |
| Freight | +0.5 |
| Gateway prices | +0.4 |
| Hardware volume | −0.4 |
| New hires | −0.3 |
| Q3 2026 | 13.1% |

Source: management accounts, Q2 and Q3 2026. Steps are rounded to 0.1 points. The axis starts at 10%.

## Speaker notes

Q2 margin was 11.8%. One step per click. Software mix is the biggest step, 1.1 points: software grew faster than hardware and earns more on each dollar. Freight added 0.5 after we moved EU orders from air to sea. The July gateway price rise added 0.4. Then two steps back: lower hardware volume cost 0.4, because fixed factory costs were spread over fewer units, and the field engineers we hired cost 0.3. We end at 13.1%, 0.3 points above plan.
