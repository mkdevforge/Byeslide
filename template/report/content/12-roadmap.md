Six workstreams for Q4. The one that matters is delivery: if the delayed gateway orders ship by 31 October, the $2.0M lands in Q4 and we stay on track for the year. The diamonds marked decision 1, 2 and 3 are the dates we need your decisions by. Pulse 4.0 brings failure alerts for pumps and ships on 12 November.
