## Speaker notes

Three asks, in the order we need them. First, the second chip supplier: $0.8M, of which $0.3M is qualification and $0.5M is buffer stock. Without it we carry the same risk into Q1. Second, moving four open hardware roles to Pulse costs nothing extra and brings compressor alerts forward by two quarters. Third, a 4% rise on gateway prices for 2027: gateways are about $10M of revenue a quarter, so this is about $1.6M a year. Sensor prices stay where they are, so we stay competitive against the low-cost makers. I'd like a decision on the first one today.
