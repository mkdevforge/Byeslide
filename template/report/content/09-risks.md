## Speaker notes

Five risks from the register, placed by how likely they are and how much they would hurt. Risk 1 is the only one high on both: if the chip stays short, Q4 hardware slips like Q3 did. The answer is a second supplier, which is the first decision on the last slide. Risks 3 and 4 are the next to watch. The others have owners and actions, and I'm not asking for anything on them today.
