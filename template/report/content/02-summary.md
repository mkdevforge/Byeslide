# Q3 revenue missed plan by $1.4M on a supply delay, while software growth lifted operating margin to 13.1%

Executive summary. The title is the governing thought; five key messages support it, each with the pages that hold the evidence. Message 1 is on screen from the start; messages 2 to 5 appear one per click.

1. **Revenue grew 12.0% to $48.6M but ended $1.4M, or 2.8%, below plan.** The gap opened in 2026. In Q3 a late controller chip held $2.0M of gateway orders, which ship in October. Pages 5 and 6.
2. **Software carried the quarter.** Software revenue rose 30.5% to $15.4M, $0.6M above plan, and we added 61 customers, the most in any quarter. Pages 6 and 7.

4. **One risk is both likely and severe: the supply of controller chips.** Q4 depends on shipping the delayed gateway orders by 31 October. Pages 11 and 12.
5. **We ask for three decisions, the first by 15 October.** A second chip supplier for $0.8M, four open roles moved to Pulse, and a 4% rise in 2027 gateway prices. Pages 14 and 15. This is the message that matters most.
