# Software revenue grew 30.5% and lifted our margin. Hardware shipments slipped, so revenue ended $1.4M below plan.

The quarter in one sentence, then the headline numbers. Revenue is the one that matters.

- **$48.6M** Revenue: 2.8% below plan, up 12.0% on Q3 2025
- **$15.4M** Software revenue: up 30.5% on Q3 2025, 4.1% above plan
- **13.1%** Operating margin: up 1.3 points on Q2 2026
- **1,056** Software customers: 52 more than at the end of Q2

Source: management accounts, close of 6 October 2026. Plan is the FY2026 budget.

## Speaker notes

If you remember one thing from today: the revenue miss is timing, not demand. $2.0M of gateway orders could not ship because a controller chip arrived five weeks late. Those orders ship in October. Software did better than plan, and because software earns more per dollar than hardware, our margin went up even with less hardware revenue.
