# We added 61 software customers in Q3, the most in any quarter, while losses held steady

Exhibit 3: New and lost software customers by quarter, number of companies. New customers above the line, lost customers below it.

| Quarter | New | Lost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q2 2025 | 38 | −7 |
| Q3 2025 | 42 | −8 |
| Q4 2025 | 47 | −6 |
| Q1 2026 | 51 | −11 |
| Q2 2026 | 55 | −9 |
| Q3 2026 | 61 | −9 |

Marker 1 is on the Q3 2026 new customers, the bar that matters. Marker 2 is on the Q1 2026 losses.

Notes:

1. **61 new customers in Q3.** New customers have grown in each of the last six quarters, from 38.
2. **Losses held at 6 to 11 a quarter.** Of the 9 lost in Q3, 6 were small sites that closed or merged.

1,056 customers on 30 September, up 20.4% on a year ago. The 9 lost in Q3 are 0.9% of the 1,004 we had on 30 June.

Source: sales CRM, 1 October 2026. A customer is a company with at least one active Pulse subscription.

## Speaker notes

New customers have grown every quarter for six quarters, from 38 to 61. Losses have stayed between 6 and 11 a quarter. Of the 9 we lost in Q3, 6 were small sites that closed or merged, and 3 moved to a cheaper competitor. Net, we ended the quarter with 52 more customers than we started with: 1,056 in total.
