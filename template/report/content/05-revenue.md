# Revenue grew 12% on last year but has trailed plan in every quarter of 2026

Exhibit 1: Revenue by quarter, actual and plan, $M

| Quarter | Actual | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q2 2025 | 41.0 | 40.5 |
| Q3 2025 | 43.4 | 43.0 |
| Q4 2025 | 46.2 | 45.5 |
| Q1 2026 | 44.1 | 44.8 |
| Q2 2026 | 46.9 | 47.2 |
| Q3 2026 | 48.6 | 50.0 |

Line labels: Plan 50.0, Actual 48.6. The point that matters is Q3 2026, marked "1.4 below plan".

What this means:

- **Demand holds.** Revenue is up 12.0% on Q3 2025.
- **The Q3 gap is timing.** Without the gateway delay, revenue would have been $0.6M above plan.
- **Q4 depends on delivery.** The delayed orders must ship by 31 October (page 12).

Source: management accounts. Plan is the budget the board approved for each year; quarters in 2025 use the FY2025 budget.

## Speaker notes

Through 2025 we ran a little ahead of plan. In 2026 we have been behind in every quarter: $0.7M in Q1, $0.3M in Q2 and $1.4M in Q3. Q3 is the gateway delay: without it we would have ended $0.6M above plan. Growth on last year is still 12%, so demand is not the problem. The question for Q4 is whether we can ship on time.
