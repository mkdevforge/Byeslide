## Speaker notes

One row per click. Revenue first, because it is the miss: $1.4M behind plan. Software revenue, margin, customers and retention are all ahead. Sensors shipped is the other miss, and it has the same cause: most sensors ship in a kit with a gateway, so when gateways wait, sensors wait too. Every measure is better than a year ago.
