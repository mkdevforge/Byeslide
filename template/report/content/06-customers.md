Source: sales CRM, 1 October 2026. A customer is a company with an active Pulse subscription.

## Speaker notes

New customers have grown every quarter for six quarters, from 38 to 61. Losses have stayed between 6 and 11 a quarter. Of the 9 we lost in Q3, 6 were small sites that closed or merged, and 3 moved to a cheaper competitor. Net, we ended the quarter with 52 more customers than we started with.
