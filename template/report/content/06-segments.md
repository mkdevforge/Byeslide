# Software beat plan by $0.6M, while a late chip left hardware $2.0M short

Exhibit 2: Revenue by segment against plan, Q3 2026, $M. Each segment shows actual, with plan.

| Marker | Segment | Actual | Plan | Against plan | On Q3 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hardware: sensors and gateways | 27.0 | Plan 29.0 | 2.0 behind | +3.4% |
| 2 | Software: Pulse subscriptions | 15.4 | Plan 14.8 | 0.6 ahead | +30.5% |
| 3 | Services: installs, calibration | 6.2 | Plan 6.2 | On plan | +12.7% |
| | Total | 48.6 | Plan 50.0 | 1.4 behind | +12.0% |

The hardware shortfall against plan is marked, and marker 1 is the one that matters.

Notes:

1. **Hardware missed by $2.0M.** Gateway orders waited for a late controller chip. They ship in October.
2. **Software beat plan by $0.6M.** Pulse subscriptions grew 30.5% on last year.
3. **Services landed on plan.** Installation and calibration grew 12.7%.

Source: management accounts, Q3 2026. Plan is the FY2026 budget. Growth compares each segment with Q3 2025.

## Speaker notes

Hardware is still more than half of our revenue, so a slip there moves the total. Marker one is the gap: $2.0M of gateway orders that were ready to sell but waited for one chip. Software came in $0.6M above plan, and services landed exactly on plan. Growth on last year: hardware 3.4%, software 30.5%, services 12.7%.
