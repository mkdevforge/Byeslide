## Speaker notes

Five dates for Q4. The one that matters is 31 October: if the delayed gateway orders ship by then, the $2.0M lands in Q4 and we stay on track for the year. Before that, on 15 October, we need the decision on a second chip supplier. Pulse 4.0 brings failure alerts for pumps, which customers have asked for since spring. Hiring should be complete by 1 December, and the FY2027 plan goes to the board on 10 December.
