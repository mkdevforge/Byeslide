# A second supplier is the only option that removes the single source, for $0.05M more than extra stock

Exhibit 8: Options for controller-chip supply, one-off cost in $M. Option A is the row that matters.

| Option | Cost | Cover | Single source removed | Risk reduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Second supplier and 8 weeks of stock (Recommended) | 0.80 | Stock from December; a second source from Q1 2027 | Yes | High |
| B. 12 weeks of stock from today's supplier | 0.75 | Stock from December | No | Partial |
| C. No change | 0.00 | None | No | None |

Recommendation:

- **Choose option A.** For $0.05M more than stock alone, it ends our dependence on one supplier.
- **Cost, $0.8M.** $0.3M to qualify the supplier and $0.5M for 8 weeks of stock.
- **Decide by 15 October** to have parts by 15 December (page 12).

Source: Operations and Finance estimates, October 2026. A week of stock costs about $62,500.

## Speaker notes

We looked at three options. Extra stock alone costs almost as much, $0.75M for 12 weeks, and leaves us with one supplier: the next shortage hits us the same way. A second supplier plus 8 weeks of stock costs $0.8M, $0.05M more, and removes the single source from Q1. Doing nothing costs nothing now and leaves the risk where it is. We recommend option A.
