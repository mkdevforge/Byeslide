Software and freight went well. Chip supply and hiring did not.

Went well

  • Software revenue up 30.5% $0.6M ahead of plan
  • 61 new software customers The most in any quarter
  • Freight cost per unit down 18% EU orders moved from air to sea

Did not go well

  • $2.0M of gateway orders slipped to October A controller chip arrived five weeks late
  • 112,000 sensors shipped, against a plan of 124,000 Most sensors ship in a kit with a gateway
  • 14 of 22 planned field engineers hired New installs now wait six weeks

Sources: management accounts, sales CRM, warehouse and HR records.

The good news is structural: software keeps growing, and the freight saving stays with us. The bad news is mostly one part. The controller chip for our gateways comes from a single supplier, and when it was five weeks late, $2.0M of orders and about 12,000 sensors waited with it. Hiring is the slower problem: we have 14 of the 22 field engineers we planned, so new customers wait six weeks for an install. Both come back in the risks and the decisions.
