Appendix: definitions and sources

Revenue
Recognized revenue in US dollars, from management accounts. Not audited.
Plan
The FY2026 budget, approved by the board in December 2025. Quarters in 2025 use the FY2025 budget.
Software revenue
Pulse subscriptions and usage fees.
Operating margin
Operating income divided by revenue.
Software customers
Companies with at least one active Pulse subscription on the last day of the quarter.
Net revenue retention
Software revenue this quarter from customers we had a year ago, divided by their software revenue a year ago.
Sensors shipped
Sensors that left our warehouses in the quarter, rounded to the nearest thousand.
Points
Percentage points, the difference between two percentages.

Sources: management accounts, close of 6 October 2026. Sales CRM, export of 1 October 2026. Warehouse shipping records to 30 September 2026. Acme Corp is a fictional company, and all names and figures in this deck are made up.
