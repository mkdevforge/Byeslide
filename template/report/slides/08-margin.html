Operating margin rose 1.3 points to 13.1%, as software mix more than offset lower hardware volume

What drove the change

  • Software mix, +1.1. Software grew faster than hardware and earns more on each dollar.
  • Freight, +0.5. EU orders moved from air to sea.
  • Gateway prices, +0.4. The July price rise.
  • Volume and hiring, −0.7. Fixed factory costs over fewer units, and new field engineers.

Source: management accounts, Q2 and Q3 2026. Steps are in percentage points, rounded to 0.1. The axis starts at 10%.

Q2 margin was 11.8%. One step per click. Software mix is the biggest step, 1.1 points: software grew faster than hardware and earns more on each dollar. Freight added 0.5 after we moved EU orders from air to sea. The July gateway price rise added 0.4. Then two steps back: lower hardware volume cost 0.4, because fixed factory costs were spread over fewer units, and the field engineers we hired cost 0.3. We end at 13.1%, 0.3 points above plan.
